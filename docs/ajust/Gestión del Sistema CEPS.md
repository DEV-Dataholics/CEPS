Within **Sistema CEPS**, the **Sistema de Exámenes** (Exam System) serves as the digital evaluation bridge connecting initial candidate profiling in the field with final operational qualification 1, 2\.

### 1\. Access via Unique Lifetime ID

* **Universal Candidate Identifier:** Upon initial field registration, the system automatically generates a unique candidate ID or QR code, which remains attached to the guard throughout their lifecycle and eventually becomes their official employee ID 3\.  
* **Public Exam Access:** Candidates access the exam platform through a dedicated public view by entering their candidate ID number or scanning their assigned QR code 2\.

### 2\. Admin Enabling & Tablet Interface Design

* **Individual Exam Assignment:** The vacancy administrator (*administrador de vacantes*) retakes candidate profiles approved from field recruitment and individually enables specific digital exams for each applicant within the platform 1, 4\.  
* **ATM-Style Interaction:** Exams are delivered digitally on tablets and operate similarly to an automated teller machine (ATM), where applicants select options with a simple touch 1, 4\.  
* **Reducing Technical Barriers:** To save time and accommodate candidates with varying levels of technological familiarity, questions are overwhelmingly click-based, with minimal open-ended text fields 4\.

### 3\. Asymmetric Visibility & Progress Monitoring

* **Candidate Confidentiality:** Candidates only record their responses and never see test scores or answer keys, though they can monitor their overall application phase on their self-service candidate portal 2, 3\.  
* **Real-Time Admin Dashboard:** Administrators have full access to inspect submitted answers and monitor live progress indicators showing which candidates have completed their tests, who is still testing, and which exams are pending 3, 4\.

### 4\. Advancement to Active Operational Status

* **Automated Profile Linking:** Test results are automatically tied to the candidate's profile, allowing administrators to determine if the applicant is qualified 2\.  
* **Assignment Readiness:** Once all assigned exams are passed and physical documentation is verified, the administrator marks the candidate as "apt" (*persona apta*), advancing them into the active pool to be assigned to client vacancies 2, 5\.
